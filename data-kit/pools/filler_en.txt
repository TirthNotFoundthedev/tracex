The incident was reported late in the evening near the market road.

Police reached the spot and inspected the site.
Neighbours were questioned but nobody gave a clear statement.
Further investigation in the matter is in progress.

The victim's phone and some cash were found missing.
Footage has been requested from a nearby shop.
