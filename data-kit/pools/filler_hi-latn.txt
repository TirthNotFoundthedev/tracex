Shikayatkarta ne bataya ki use anjaan logon ne raaste mein roka tha.
Mauke par pahunchkar police ne sthal ka nirikshan kiya.
Aaspaas ke logon se poochhtaachh ki gayi, kisi ne spasht bayan nahi diya.
Prakaran mein aage ki jaanch jaari hai.
Ghatnasthal se kuchh dastavez baraamad kiye gaye.
Peedit ka mobile phone aur kuchh nakad raashi gayab payi gayi.
Paas ki dukaan se footage ki maang ki gayi hai.
Bayan darj kar liye gaye hain aur gawahon ke hastakshar karwaye gaye.
Shikayat milte hi prarambhik jaanch shuru ki gayi.
