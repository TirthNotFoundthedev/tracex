## Your goal

## What to send back

For each thing you tried, write four lines:

- **Tried:** what you did.
- **Expected:** what you thought would happen.
- **Happened:** what you saw on the screen.
- **Stuck:** where you got stuck or confused. Write "none" if you did not.

Add a severity guess for each problem: **blocker** (you could not go on), **major** (you went on, but it was hard or wrong), or **minor** (small annoyance).

Finish with three lines: could you reach your goal in five minutes (yes / partly / no), how long it really took, and what slowed you down most.
